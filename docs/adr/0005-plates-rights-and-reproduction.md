# ADR 0005 · Keep every figure now; reproduce publisher figures from data later

Status: accepted, 2026-09-26 (decision 7).

Decision: all figures from theses and articles are cropped locally as plates. Own-thesis plates are `rights: own` (CC BY 4.0). Plates from publisher PDFs are `rights: publisher`, `reproduce: pending`; they are kept in the repository as reference for reproduction from data and are not distributed on the public site until reproduced (`rights: reproduced`) or cleared with the journal. The plates gallery is therefore also the reproduction queue.
